What to watch: Keep the test exactly as good as it is and drag the base rate down. When the condition is rare, the healthy crowd is so enormous that even a small false-positive rate produces a huge false-positive count — gold squares swamp red ones among the positives. The test didn't change; the population did. That is the base rate doing the work most intuitions leave out.
