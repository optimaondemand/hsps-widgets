What to watch: The gold triangle is the expected value — the point where the distribution would balance if each bar were a weight on a seesaw. Try the raffle ticket: almost all the probability piles up at −$1, yet one far-away outcome drags the balance point to about 0. Then switch on the spread: two distributions can balance at the same place while one sits far wider than the other.
