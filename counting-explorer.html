What to watch: In arrangements mode, ABC and CBA count separately — order matters, and the count is a falling product: n × (n−1) × …. Switch to selections mode and the grid collapses: every group of r! orderings shrinks to a single selection, which is why nCr = nPr ÷ r!. Click any selection to see exactly which arrangements collapsed into it. Then nudge n upward and watch the factorial panel — adding one item doesn't add to the count, it multiplies it.
