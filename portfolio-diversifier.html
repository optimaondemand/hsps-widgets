What to watch: Slide k upward. The gold EV line never moves — splitting the stake cannot change the expected gain of $10, because expectation adds up no matter what. But the spread collapses like 1/√k: independent bets rarely all go wrong at once, so the disasters and the jackpots cancel toward the middle. Diversification is the rare free lunch — same expected reward, much less risk.
