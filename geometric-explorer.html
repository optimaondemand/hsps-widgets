Navy bars: simulated counts. dots: the theoretical p(1−p)k−1 prediction.
What to watch: The histogram always starts tall at k = 1 — the single most likely wait is one flip! — and then trails off in a long right tail. Most waits are short, but a few drag on far past the average, which is why the mean wait (1/p) sits well to the right of the most common wait. Changing p doesn’t change the shape of the story; it only stretches or squeezes the tail.
