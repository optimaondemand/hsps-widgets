What to watch: In with replacement mode, every drawn marble goes back, so the urn — and the probability readout — never changes: the draws are independent. Switch to without replacement and draw a few times: each marble you remove changes the mix, and P(black on the next draw) moves after every single draw. That movement is dependence — the past whispering to the future.
