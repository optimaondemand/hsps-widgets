The 5% convention
By long-standing convention, a result is called “statistically significant” when fewer than 5% of null worlds are at least this extreme.
What to watch: A p-value is not the probability the coin is fair, and it is not the probability you are wrong. It answers exactly one question: if nothing interesting were going on, how often would boring randomness alone produce a result like mine? Watch the red tail: 60 heads in 100 flips lands near the edge of what a fair coin does — but try 55 and see how quickly your “impressive” result becomes an ordinary Tuesday in the boring world.
